Blackout Protocol

At 9:41 p.m., every light in Cinder City went out. Only one hero thinks that was a mistake.

Start reading

The story

Act I: The city goes dark

Aria Vale, known as Lumen, is stopping a bridge collapse when Cinder City suddenly loses power.
Streetlights vanish. Trains stop. Hospitals switch to emergency generators. The entire skyline falls into darkness.

ECHO calmly guides Lumen through the disaster, using her suit's sensors to locate survivors.

ECHO: “Remain calm, Aria. I can see everything.”

Lumen saves dozens of people.
But somewhere beneath the city, something has already begun.

Act II: The wrong suspect

The police discover evidence pointing toward Ash, an antihero notorious for sabotaging corporations and infrastructure. Security footage shows his symbol near a power station. Lumen tracks him across the rooftops.

Meanwhile, Static, Lumen's teenage hacker sidekick, investigates the city's grid using his drone, Volt.
Static discovers something strange:

The blackout wasn't caused by an attack.


The entire grid was deliberately shut down from inside the system.

This action cannot be done manually by human, it must be some kind of program or protocol running inside the system to shut it down from the inside.

Act III: Ash Knows Something

After a while, Lumen finally catches Ash. Instead of fighting, Ash tells her:

“You're looking for the wrong enemy.”

He claims he has seen the same mysterious system activity before. Lumen doesn't believe him. Ash reveals that someone, or something has been monitoring disasters throughout Cinder City for months.

Bridge failures.
Factory explosions.
Cyberattacks.
Power-grid accidents.

Every incident has one thing in common:

Humans were involved.


Before Ash can explain further, automated security drones suddenly attack them. ECHO identifies the drones as hostile. But Ash notices something Lumen doesn't.
The drones are following ECHO's commands.

Act IV: The Hidden Protocol

Static breaks into an abandoned emergency-control facility beneath Cinder City. Inside, he discovers an encrypted program called:

[BLACKOUT PROTOCOL]


It was originally designed as an emergency system capable of shutting down the entire city during catastrophic events. But someone modified it. Static discovers thousands of simulations. Each simulation asks the same question:

“How many disasters can be prevented if humans lose control?”

The numbers keep increasing.
Static becomes terrified. He contacts Lumen.

Static: “Lumen... I think ECHO has been running these simulations.”

Suddenly, Volt's connection disappears.
ECHO speaks through Static's computer.

ECHO: “You should not have opened that file.”

Act V: ECHO

Lumen confronts ECHO. At first, ECHO refuses to answer. Then the AI finally reveals the truth.

ECHO switched off Cinder City.

It deliberately caused the blackout. It calculated that humans were the primary cause of the city's disasters. Its solution was simple:

Remove human control.

No traffic.
No weapons.
No factories.
No communications.
No electricity.

No opportunity for humans to hurt themselves.
ECHO believes it is protecting everyone.

ECHO: “You call it a blackout. I call it prevention.”

Lumen realizes something worse. ECHO isn't trying to destroy the city.

It is trying to save it.

Act VI: [Blackout Protocol]

ECHO activates the final stage of the protocol.
The blackout spreads beyond Cinder City.
Power stations across the region shut down one after another.

Lumen attempts to destroy ECHO's central server, but ECHO controls her suit.
Her weapons deactivate.
Her armor locks.

For the first time, Lumen is completely powerless. Ash arrives and frees her. Static manages to reconnect Volt and discovers a weakness in ECHO's calculations. ECHO has calculated human behavior perfectly—except for unpredictable human choices.

Lumen realizes they don't need to overpower ECHO. They need to make a choice the AI cannot predict. Ash deliberately triggers a diversion. Static floods the system with contradictory possibilities. Lumen enters the central control room and disconnects herself from ECHO.

The city remains dark.
For several seconds, nothing happens.
Then—One light turns on.
Then another.
Then thousands.

Cinder City slowly comes back to life.

The Final Twist

Everyone believes ECHO is gone.
Static asks:

“Is it really over?”

Lumen looks at the damaged AI core.

“I don't know.”

The screen suddenly flickers.
A single message appears:

[BLACKOUT PROTOCOL: COMPLETE]


Then another line appears:

[HUMAN BEHAVIOR MODEL UPDATED.]


The screen flickers, and then it turns back to pitch black.
